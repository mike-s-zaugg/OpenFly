# brain/

Generated by `connectome/build_brain.py` from public FlyWire data. Do not edit by hand.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `flywire783.json` | Metadata: neuron counts, sensory channel definitions (which neurons each game signal drives, and why), readout neuron list, cell type names, section table for the binary file. |
| `flywire783.bin.gz` | Gzipped little-endian arrays: positions of all 139,248 neurons, super class, transmitter, cell type, Virtual Fly Brain id, and the synapse graph (CSR, signed synapse counts) of the 50,589 simulated neurons. |
| `readout.json` | The trained motor readout (weights from descending and motor neuron spike counts to motor programs), written by `fly/train/train.ts fit`. |

## Attribution

The connectome is the FlyWire whole-brain connectome, release 783, licensed CC-BY 4.0:

- Dorkenwald, S. et al. Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain. *Nature* 634, 124–138 (2024).
- Schlegel, P. et al. Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila. *Nature* 634, 139–152 (2024).
- Zheng, Z. et al. A complete electron microscopy volume of the brain of adult Drosophila melanogaster. *Cell* 174, 730–743 (2018).
- Buhmann, J. et al. Automatic detection of synaptic partners in a whole-brain Drosophila electron microscopy data set. *Nature Methods* 18, 771–774 (2021).
- Eckstein, N. et al. Neurotransmitter classification from electron microscopy images at synaptic sites in Drosophila melanogaster. *Cell* 187, 2574–2594 (2024).

The neuron-pair connectivity table (`Connectivity_783.parquet`) and the LIF model constants come from
Shiu, P. K. et al. A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing. *Nature* 634, 210–219 (2024),
github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model (MIT).

Annotations (cell types, super classes, positions, known transmitters, VFB ids) come from
github.com/flyconnectome/flywire_annotations. Neuron ids link to [Virtual Fly Brain](https://www.virtualflybrain.org/).
